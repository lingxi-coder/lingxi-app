# Friends

Add a friend, chat one-to-one, share a Bot with a friend or invite a friend to a team.

<a id="add-friend"></a>

## Add a friend

1. Open Friends → Profile settings, set a display name and an optional username, and choose whether people may find you by username or by email.
2. Choose Add friend, enter an exact @username, an email address or a friend link and choose Look up. Add an optional note and send the request. To add someone from a shared team, open the team details → Members and choose Add friend on that member.

- [Open Friends](https://lingxios.net/friends)

Only an exact username or a verified email finds you, and your email address is never shown. Every miss looks the same.

<a id="friend-link"></a>

## Share your friend link

Under Add friend → My friend link choose Copy link or Share link. Anyone who opens it and confirms becomes your friend right away, without your approval. Reset link stops the old link immediately; Turn off link disables it until you create a new one.

<a id="requests"></a>

## Answer friend requests

Friend requests → Received lists requests to Accept or Decline; Sent lets you Cancel request. If the other person already asked you, sending your own request accepts theirs. You can have 50 outgoing requests and 500 friends.

<a id="block"></a>

## Remove or block someone

In the friend’s menu choose Remove friend or Block. Both end the friendship and delete the chat for both people. A blocked person cannot find you or send requests until you unblock them in Blocked people. Unblocking does not restore the friendship.

<a id="friend-bots"></a>

## Share a Bot with a friend

Open the friend chat → Shared Bots. To share one of your Bots, use LingXi Desktop on the Mac that runs it, choose Share a Bot with the friend, review and confirm. The friend gets one Bot DM per Bot. The Bot runs on its maintainer’s Desktop and the maintainer can read every conversation with it. Stop sharing blocks new requests and keeps history.

- [Other ways to share a Bot](bots.md#audience)

<a id="team-offer"></a>

## Invite a friend to a team

A team owner or admin opens the team details → Invite friends, picks a current friend and chooses Invite. The friend sees the team name and your name under Team invitations in Friends and can Join or Dismiss. Nothing else is shared until they join, always as a member. The invitation expires after 7 days.

- [Team invite links](teams.md#invite)
